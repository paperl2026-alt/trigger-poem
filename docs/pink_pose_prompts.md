# 粉发枪娘 · 战斗姿势出图提示词（给 GPT 用）

用法：每次把 **设定图（正面+背面那张）** 作为参考图一起发给 GPT，再贴下面一段提示词。一次出一张（每张两个人物）。
出好的图直接发我，我负责切图、对位、做晃动和枪口位置。

要求（每张都一样，已经写进提示词里）：
- 透明背景或纯白背景；两个人物同一比例、同一高度基准，中间留宽空隙，不要互相挡、不要被裁切。
- 不要掩体、不要地面、不要枪口火焰和特效（游戏里会自己加）。
- 她在屏幕**右侧**（左边站的是猫娘），所以镜头在她身后偏右，她朝画面深处、略向左。

---

## 通用开头（每张都先贴这段）

```
Use the attached character design sheet: keep her face, hair, twin tails, outfit, shoes and gun exactly the same.
Game character sprite sheet: two full-body figures of the same girl side by side, identical scale, a wide empty gap between them, nothing overlapping or cut off,
plain white or transparent background, no floor, no shadow, no cover objects, no muzzle flash, no effects.
Camera: directly BEHIND her and slightly to her right at shoulder height — the over-the-shoulder rear view of the heroines in NIKKE: Goddess of Victory.
She stands on the RIGHT side of the screen, facing away from the viewer into the depth of the picture, turned slightly to the LEFT; her face only in lost profile.
Polished anime gacha-game art, clean lineart, soft cel shading.
```

## 1. 掩体 + 射击
```
Left figure, TAKING COVER: crouched low on one knee, hugging the gun upright to her chest, head turned left showing a sly profile, twin tails pooling down her back.
Right figure, FIRING: standing in a wide stance, leaning forward slightly, gun shouldered and aimed into the distance a little left of straight ahead, twin tails swinging.
```
## 2. 向外侧瞄准 + 后坐
```
Left figure, AIMING OUTWARD: gun shouldered and aimed toward the upper RIGHT of the picture, body twisted right.
Right figure, RECOIL: aiming forward, the gun kicking hard, shoulders jolted, twin tails whipping.
```
## 3. 受击 + 换弹
```
Left figure, HIT REACTION: recoiling backward from a blow, one forearm raised, head turned showing a pained profile.
Right figure, RELOADING IN COVER: crouched on one knee, slapping a fresh drum magazine into the gun, head turned with a cheeky smile.
```
## 4. 跑步（两帧）
```
Left figure, RUNNING away from the viewer into the depth of the picture, seen from behind, mid-stride with the right leg forward, gun in one hand, twin tails streaming.
Right figure, RUNNING, the other stride: left leg forward, arms pumping.
```
## 5. 探头 + 挑衅
```
Left figure, PEEK: crouched, seen from the side and slightly behind, rising to look forward, gun ready, eyes narrowed. (No barricade drawn.)
Right figure, TAUNT: crouched, turning her upper body back toward the viewer with a big grin and a peace sign, gun resting on her shoulder.
```
## 6. 倒地 + 胜利
```
Left figure, KNOCKED DOWN: sitting on the floor after a fall, seen from the side, one hand on the floor, dizzy.
Right figure, VICTORY facing the viewer (front view): gun raised high overhead in one hand, other hand making a heart sign, winking.
```
## 7. 必杀（加特林扫射）
```
Left figure, SPECIAL ATTACK: her gun has unfolded into a bigger triple-barrel rotary gatling with bunny charms; she braces it at her hip with both hands and sprays forward into the distance a little left of straight ahead, leaning into the recoil.
Right figure, SPECIAL ATTACK toward the upper RIGHT: the same gatling braced at her hip, swung toward the upper right of the picture.
```

## 8. 头像（一张四个）
```
Use the attached character design sheet: keep her face and hair exactly.
Four head-and-shoulders portraits of the same girl in a single row, all facing the viewer at identical scale, heads at the same height, a wide gap between them, cut off cleanly at mid-chest,
plain white or transparent background, no frame.
1: sweet sly smile. 2: startled, eyes wide. 3: fired-up, fierce grin. 4: hurt, wincing with one eye shut.
```
## 9. 必杀切入（横幅）
```
Use the attached character design sheet: keep her exactly.
Wide cinematic cut-in illustration for a special-attack moment, a very wide horizontal banner about 3:1, plain white or transparent background, no effects:
her from the waist up, front view, leaning toward the viewer with a wild grin, firing the triple-barrel gatling straight at the viewer, twin tails flaring.
```
